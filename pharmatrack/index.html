How PharmaTrack Works

A data and intelligence layer on top of pharmacies you already trust

PharmaTrack does not replace pharmacy management systems. It imports the exports they already produce and turns scattered spreadsheets into one searchable, always-labeled-for-freshness picture of medicine availability.

1. Pharmacies export inventory

Existing legacy systems export inventory as CSV — no new hardware or software required on the pharmacy side.

2. PharmaTrack cleans & normalizes

Different column names, casing, and formats are validated, cleaned, and matched against one shared medicine catalog.

3. Citizens search, pharmacies manage, admins monitor

The public sees fresh, honestly-labeled availability. Pharmacies get a dashboard. Admins get system-wide analytics and shortage signals.
