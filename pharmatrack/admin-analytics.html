System-wide Analytics

Shortage & Availability Intelligence

Eight views into medicine availability, demand, and shortage risk across the entire PharmaTrack network — open to the public for transparency.

1 · Medicine Availability Over Time

Average inventory quantity across all sampled pharmacy-medicine pairs.

2 · Top Medicines by Search Activity

Most searched medicines (public search interest).

3 · Most Frequently Out-of-Stock

Share of tracking pharmacies reporting zero stock.

4 · Availability by City

Inventory record counts by status, per city.

5 · Availability by Medicine Category

Inventory record counts by status, per category.

6 · Pharmacy Data Freshness

How recently pharmacies last synchronized.

7 · Inventory Trend

Total quantity across sampled history records, by month.

8 · Shortage Trend Over Time

Percentage of historical snapshots recorded at zero stock, by month.

Shortage Risk — Demo Prediction

Rule-based composite of out-of-stock ratio, inventory decline trend, recent search volume, and pharmacy breadth. A future release replaces this with a scikit-learn model (Logistic Regression / Random Forest) trained on real historical outcomes.
